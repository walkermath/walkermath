    <p><b>Population.</b> Tap cards to add them to or remove them from the population. Each card's value is its number: ace = 1, J/Q/K = 10. The histogram shows the population's distribution with its mean μ and standard deviation σ.</p>
    <p><b>Samples.</b> Choose a sample size n and draw. Each sample draws n cards <i>with replacement</i> (each card goes back before the next draw) and records the sample mean x̄. Every x̄ goes into the sampling distribution histogram.</p>
    <p><b>Predict, then reveal.</b> Compare the mean and SD of your x̄'s with what theory says: mean = μ and standard error = σ/√n.</p>
    <p><b>z-scores.</b> Turn on <i>show z-scores</i> to see z = (x̄ − μ) / (σ/√n) for each sample. Samples with |z| &gt; 2 are flagged.</p>
