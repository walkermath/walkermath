    <p><b>Distribution.</b> Z is the standard normal. X ~ N(μ, σ) is any normal model, like test scores. x̄ ~ N(μ, σ/√n) is the sampling distribution of a sample mean: by the Central Limit Theorem its spread is the standard error σ/√n.</p>
